# About

<!-- Placeholder: three lines, to be replaced. -->

digline is an open-source tool for keeping LLM applications under control, released under Apache-2.0.

It came out of shipping such applications and needing to know, on every change, whether they had got worse.

The code, the issues and the releases are on [GitHub](https://github.com/digline/digline).
